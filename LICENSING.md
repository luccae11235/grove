# Licensing Aflora

Aflora uses separate licenses for software and original publication content.
This file defines which license applies when a repository path does not include
a more specific license or notice.

## Software — MIT

Original source code, tests, build scripts, configuration, and technical
documentation in this repository are licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
Package-level license files continue to govern their package when present.

## Publication content — CC BY 4.0

Unless a file or article says otherwise, original material in these locations is
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT.md):

- `apps/web/src/content/articles/` — original article prose and authored examples;
- `apps/web/src/content/issues/` — original newsletter issue prose;
- `apps/web/public/articles/` — original educational illustrations and diagrams.

The license applies only to original expression owned by Lucca Emmanuel. Source
citations, dataset credits, and article-specific notices remain in force.

## Not licensed under these grants

The following are excluded unless an explicit notice says otherwise:

- the Aflora name, wordmark, logo, and files under `apps/web/public/brand/`;
- social cards and other assets that incorporate the Aflora brand;
- third-party text, images, code, fonts, or other materials;
- cited datasets, externally sourced observations, and derived data that remain
  subject to their source terms;
- names and marks belonging to their respective owners.

The licenses do not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement. When a file
combines original Aflora material with third-party material, the applicable
license covers only the original Aflora portion.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you agree that your software contribution is
licensed under MIT and that a contribution to the publication-content paths
listed above is licensed under CC BY 4.0, unless the contribution is accompanied
by a different notice accepted by the project owner.
