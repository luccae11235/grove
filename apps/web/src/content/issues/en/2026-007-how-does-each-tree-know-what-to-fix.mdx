One decision tree tries to solve a prediction problem in one pass. Gradient
boosting asks a different question: what small correction would improve the
model we already have?

Begin with one constant prediction. Measure what remains wrong. Train a small
tree to generalize that correction, add only part of it, and repeat.

In this week's interactive article, you can build that sequence yourself for
regression and classification. You will see why residual fitting is one special
case of a broader idea: gradient descent in the space of predictive functions.
