Look at the line below. Its peaks seem to return on a schedule.

![Daily sales rise and fall in a repeating pattern, with similar peaks returning at regular intervals.](https://aflora.page/articles/acf-pacf/m5-ca1-daily-sales-2015-q1.svg)

Finding repetition is easy. The harder question is whether an older value tells
us something useful—or only looks important because the same pattern keeps
repeating.

Knowing the difference helps people who build predictions decide how much
history to trust and avoid mistaking an echo for new information.

In the new interactive article, you'll shift the data through time, uncover the
relationships yourself, and build an intuitive understanding of the tools
experts call ACF and PACF.
