Suppose today's value in a time series looks related to yesterday's. Is that a
direct relationship, or is yesterday only carrying an influence that began even
earlier?

The new interactive article starts with ordinary correlation, then shifts a
series through time to make each lag visible. You can construct the observation
pairs yourself, compare autocorrelation across lags, and see why a relationship
can persist after its direct influence has ended.

By the end, you will have an intuitive way to distinguish what the ACF and PACF
measure—and to read their shapes as clues rather than causal proof.
